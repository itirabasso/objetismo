objetismo

Rebuilds an image out of Argentum Online item sprites. It all runs in your browser; the image never leaves your device.

Image
Each cell becomes the item whose color, on the black background, is closest in CIELAB. The brightest tile has a lightness of about 68 out of 100, so light images come out dimmer.

Zoom with the mouse wheel, the buttons or + − 0 1, and drag to move around. Hover a tile to see its item.
